Chapter 01 · Where it began

My story starts in my neighbourhood.

My name is Ibrahim Isah, though many know me as Khalifa. Raised in Kano State, Nigeria, this is my story—a journey of continuous learning, resilience, and growth.

🏡

My roots

The neighbourhood where I grew up is where I attended school. My background has shaped the way I see life, hard work, and the value of every opportunity.

🌱

Still growing

I am still learning about myself and the world. I believe that a difficult chapter does not have to be the end of a person's story.

Chapter 02 · Lessons and challenges

My education journey

I attended primary and secondary school in my community. Because of difficult circumstances in life, I could not complete secondary school. That was a challenge, but it did not end my curiosity or my desire to learn.

📚

School days

I began my education in my neighbourhood, where I attended primary and secondary school.

⚡

A dream of engineering

I hoped to study electrical and electronics engineering. I spent time learning about the field, even though circumstances made it difficult to continue through the usual path.

🌟

Learning beyond the classroom

I continue to explore ideas, research topics, and teach myself new skills with the tools available to me.

“My path has not been easy, but I still have the chance to learn, create, and move forward.”

🎨

Photo and video editing

I like working with images and video, experimenting with visual ideas, and turning information into content people can enjoy.

Chapter 04 · Creating something of my own

My journey with World Origins 🌍

I began thinking about creating videos. At first, I considered making content about electrical and electronics topics. Later, I chose a wider idea: sharing interesting things about the world. That idea became World Origins.

🌎

A world of discoveries

World Origins shares short educational videos about places, people, brands, landmarks, inventions, and other interesting facts from around the world.

📱

Four platforms

I share my work on Facebook, TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube, learning as I create and paying attention to what viewers enjoy.

One of my proudest moments was seeing a video project grow beyond what I had imagined. As I understood TikTok's monetisation requirements at the time, I needed to reach 10,000 followers and 100,000 video views within 30 days. Within those 28 days, I achieved those targets—and went far beyond them. My account grew to approximately 11,000 followers and 4.5 million video views. I was incredibly happy and hoped this achievement could help me move closer to my dreams.

However, when I tried to complete the identity verification needed to access monetisation, I encountered difficulties because I did not have the accepted identification documents required at that time. It was disappointing, but the skills and experience I gained through World Origins still have value. My journey did not end there; I continued looking for new ways to learn, create, and build a better future.

Chapter 05 · Everyday effort

My family and daily life

I am the youngest in my household. The people around me have followed different paths of education and work, and their efforts are part of the world I come from.

🤝

Working and helping

Sometimes I join my older brother in road asphalt and concrete work. The money I earn helps with everyday needs such as phone credit, mobile data, and other small expenses.

🧰

Learning from family

One of my older brothers works with industrial machines, and one of my older sisters teaches religious studies. Seeing different kinds of work reminds me that everyone has a role to play.

Chapter 06 · A new skill

Discovering web development

After facing difficulties with online creator earnings, I began exploring web development on my own. Without a personal teacher guiding every step, I started researching, experimenting, and learning how websites are made.

From an idea to a website

​I have already learned how to build websites, and I am continuing to improve my skills. Creating a special website for someone very important to me was a way to show love, care, and appreciation ❤️, while learning how technology can be used to tell a story.

Chapter 07 · Finding my voice

My journey with English

I can write in English, but speaking and understanding spoken English can be difficult for me. I am practising step by step because I want to understand my friends, reply with confidence, and communicate more naturally.

🎧

Listening and speaking

I practise with beginner-friendly lessons, listen carefully, repeat useful expressions, and work on speaking without rushing myself.

📝

Small steps count

I know that progress takes time. Each new word, conversation, and practice session is another step toward my goal.

Chapter 08 · A meaningful friendship

My story with Aisha ❤️

I first noticed Aisha's messages in an English-learning WhatsApp group on 5 October 2026. On 6 October, I started a conversation with her. I was happy with the way she welcomed me with kindness and respect, and I felt grateful to have found someone I could talk with and learn from.

💬

A kind beginning

Our conversations became meaningful to me. I appreciated her character and the kindness she showed me, and I was glad our paths had crossed.

🤲

Prayers from the heart

When I learned that Aisha was going through a difficult time, I sincerely wished for her wellbeing. For three consecutive days, I woke at around 3:00 a.m. and prayed to Allah to ease her difficulties, protect her, and guide her toward better days.

Aisha, you were never a burden to me because you shared your feelings. I hope you never feel that you must hide your worries to protect someone else's feelings. You deserve to be heard, supported, and treated with kindness.

I know I cannot solve every problem for someone else, but I can listen when appropriate, offer encouragement, and pray for good things. My hope is that Aisha will keep believing that difficult moments do not define her whole life, and that she can seek support whenever she needs it.

Chapter 09 · The road ahead
